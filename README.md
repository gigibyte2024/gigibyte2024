## Featured Projects 🚀

### [EngageIQ AI — Agentic Classroom Engagement Monitor](https://github.com/newton-school-ai/engageiq-ai_2)

An AI-powered system that analyses classroom engagement through signals such as drowsiness, distraction, gaze and phone usage. Combines these signals into an engagement score and uses an agentic workflow to suggest personalized interventions. Designed with on-device vision processing to support privacy.

**Focus:** Computer Vision · Agentic AI · Real-time Analysis

### [Jal Netra — Urban Flood Intelligence Platform](https://github.com/slayplayagrima/JalNetra)

An urban flood intelligence platform that estimates location-level flood risk using rainfall forecasts, GIS and elevation data, drainage information and historical flooding. Its workflow verifies predictions against visual and citizen-reported evidence to support local alerts, alternate routes and response planning.

**Focus:** AI/ML · Geospatial Intelligence · Risk Prediction

### [FDF — Real-Time Group Ordering Platform](https://github.com/DevLin27/FDF)

A real-time group-ordering platform that matches nearby users with similar carts to help meet Blinkit's minimum order value. Includes live matchmaking, room management and Google sign-in.

**Focus:** Full-Stack Development · WebSockets · System Design
